Finch's Home

a small place for small birds

Finches are tiny, seed-eating songbirds with stout conical bills and cheerful, chattering calls. They travel in loose flocks, flashing bright patches of red, yellow, and rose as they bound from branch to branch in that distinctive dipping flight.

From the scarlet house finch at the feeder to the goldfinch in the thistle, to Darwin's own finches on the Galápagos — each beak shaped by the seed it cracks.
