Owl's Hollow

a quiet place for night watchers

Owls are silent-winged hunters of the dusk, their soft-edged feathers muffling flight to a whisper. Huge forward-facing eyes gather every scrap of moonlight, while asymmetric ears triangulate the rustle of a mouse beneath the snow.

From the great horned owl on a pine bough to the tiny saw-whet tucked into a cedar, to the barn owl drifting ghost-pale across the hayfield — each one shaped by silence, patience, and the dark.
